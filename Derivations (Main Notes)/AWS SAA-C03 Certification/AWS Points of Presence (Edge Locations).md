AWS has 400+ Points of Presence (400 + Edge Locations & 10+ Regional Caches) in 90+ cities across 40+ countries. This is helpful to deliver content to end users with the lowest latency manageable.

An AWS Points of Presence (PoP) is a largely distributed network location, separate from AWS Regions, designed to bring AWS services closer to end-users to reduce latency and improve performance. These have been strategically placed in major cities to cache popular content (like images or videos) nearer to users.
