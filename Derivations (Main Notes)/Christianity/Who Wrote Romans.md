All critical biblical scholars agree on the Pauline authorship for the book of romans. This means the apostle Paul, or someone whom he appointed to write on his behalf, wrote the book of Romans. The date of this books origin is believed to be in 57 A.D, near the end of his third missionary journey. This is evidently during his three month stay in Greece (Acts 20:3-6), more precisely, while he was in Corinth
