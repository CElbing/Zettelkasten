''I long to see you so that I may impart to you some spiritual gift to make you strong - that is, that you and I may be mutually encouraged by each other's faith."

I really liked this scripture as it illustrates that Paul wished to strengthen the faith of the people of Rome. However, he will also grow in faith from strengthening their faith. I believe this is an important and missed message within Romans. When you see others strong in faith, on fire for the Lord, you too will have your spirit lit ablaze. This compounds as you and those around you grow in faith, producing strong and impactful Christians. 

One of the most common and impactful ways God speaks to you is through others. This is a perfect example of that, and how just one man, the Apostle Paul, can use his faith to strengthen the faith of an empire as mighty as the Roman Empire, and eventually the whole world.
